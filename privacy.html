Privacy Policy — Hikayat Madhi

Last updated: September 13, 2026

What this app does

“Hikayat Madhi” is a personal automation that publishes videos created by its owner to the owner’s own social-media accounts (YouTube and TikTok). It is not offered to other users.

Data we access

  • TikTok: with the owner’s consent, the app requests the OAuth scopes video.upload and video.publish to upload videos and manage posts on the owner’s account. No other TikTok data is read, and no data from other users is accessed.
  • YouTube: equivalent scopes are used to upload the owner’s videos.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored exclusively in the private, access-controlled storage of the automation environment (GitHub Actions). They are never displayed, sold, or shared with third parties. Generated videos contain no personal data.

Data sharing & deletion

No user data is shared with any third party. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or YouTube (myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access), which immediately deletes all stored tokens.
